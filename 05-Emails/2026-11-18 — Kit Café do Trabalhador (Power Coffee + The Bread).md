## The idea

One Brazilian talking to others: Leo knows the routine. You leave the house in the dark, eat badly or not at all, live on gas-station coffee, and hit the wall at 2pm with half the day still left. In Brazil there was a proper café da manhã (breakfast). Here there's no time.

The offer is a **"Kit Café do Trabalhador"**: Power Coffee plus The Bread's salted granola, a breakfast that fits in a backpack and takes 2 minutes. The angle is pride and family, not "productivity": *get home with energy left for your kids*. That is Leo's own story (the crash was stealing time from his family).

## Subject lines (A/B)
- A: **Você começa às 6. Seu café também deveria.** (You start at 6. Your coffee should too.)
- B: **Pra quem trabalha pesado e ainda quer chegar inteiro em casa** (For people who work hard and still want to get home in one piece)
- Preview text: *Um café da manhã de brasileiro, que cabe na mochila.* (A Brazilian breakfast that fits in your backpack.)

## Why it should land
- **Recognition first, product second.** The first lines describe their day, not the coffee.
- **Saudade:** "no Brasil a gente tinha café da manhã de verdade" is a real, emotional anchor.
- **Family over hustle:** it matches Leo's founder story and avoids "work more" messaging, which this audience is tired of.
- **Practical:** it fits in a backpack, takes 2 minutes and is cheaper than daily gas-station coffee ($1.59 a dose vs. a $3–4 coffee: check before using).
- **The forward P.S.** suits a community that shares by WhatsApp and word of mouth. Pair it with a WhatsApp-ready version (below).
