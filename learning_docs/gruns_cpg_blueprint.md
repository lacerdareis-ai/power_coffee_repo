# Grüns CPG Blueprint — How to Build a $1B Exit in 32 Months
**Article:** The Food Stack | Date Analyzed: Jun 23, 2026

---

## Core Problem Grüns Solved
**The Gap:** Supplement consumption habits fail, not awareness or formulation.

People buy supplements. They take them for 2 weeks. They stop.
- 90% household penetration in supplements overall
- But only 58% for general health, 29% for immunity
- Gap between intent and consistent behavior is enormous

**Grüns' Insight:** The friction point is the daily ritual, not the nutrition.
- Problem: Powder formats require mixing, cleanup, chalky taste
- Solution: Format that's so convenient and enjoyable that stopping feels strange
- Result: Habit formation drives category, not product claims

**Power Coffee Alignment:**
✅ Already solved the ritual friction (instant + tastes good)
✅ Convertible format (coffee is already a daily ritual)
✅ Convenience is built-in (add to existing coffee)
⚠️ Need to emphasize the ritual lock, not just the ingredients

## The Seven Grüns Decisions That Mattered

### 1. Start with Behavioral Problem, Not Formulation Problem
**Grüns approach:** Why do people stop taking supplements they know work?
**Not:** What nutrition do people need?

The question reframes the entire business.
- Behavioral failures = market opportunity
- Format matters more than ingredient list for adoption
- Taste is king (supplement that tastes like medicine fails regardless of contents)

**Application to Power Coffee:**
- Already positioned around the crash problem (behavioral)
- Lean into ritual lock + habit formation
- Emphasize taste surprise (300/70% testers comment: "shocked" quality)
- Marketing angle: "the supplement people actually enjoy taking"

---

### 2. Perfect the Product Before Launch (12 Months)
**Grüns execution:**
- 12 months of R&D before launch (August 2023)
- Tested with 25% of Stanford cohort
- Worked through 20 co-manufacturers
- Iterated until taste was genuinely good

**Why this matters:**
- Taste creates organic sharing (people took gummies to run clubs)
- Reviews surprised consumers (expected medicine, got something they wanted)
- First customer cohort became free marketers

**Application to Power Coffee:**
- Already done: 2+ years of formulation refinement before market
- 430 mornings of self-testing by Leo
- Already achieving the "shocked by quality" response (300/70% data)
- Lean into the surprise + shareability angle
- Film testimonials about how surprised they were by taste

---

### 3. Maintain DTC-First, Even After Retail
**Grüns approach:**
- Stayed DTC-majority throughout 32 months
- Subscription model = customers use 2+ times/week
- Direct feedback channel preserved (customers have strong opinions)
- Made 5 distinct product changes in year 1 based on feedback

**Why this matters:**
- Wholesale relationships hide signal (retailers don't give real usage data)
- Subscription model ensures high-frequency usage + engagement
- Hard to replicate this feedback channel at scale

**Application to Power Coffee:**
- Currently selling direct + retail
- Opportunity: Strengthen subscription model
- Action: Emphasize the ritual subscription ("Boosted days every morning")
- Data: Use customer feedback to iterate (already doing with LENS)
- Pricing: $17.90/pack could become $X/month subscription for better retention

---

### 4. Avatar Specificity in Messaging
**Grüns approach:**
- Built multiple versions of landing pages
- Same product, different positioning per customer profile
- Example: messaging shifted between fitness, gut health, convenience angles
- Each audience saw the problem that mattered to them

**Why this matters:**
- Increased conversion + retention at premium price ($80/month)
- Allows single product to address multiple markets
- Builds stronger relationship (audience feels seen)

**Application to Power Coffee:**
- Already doing with 6 pillars (P1-P6)
- Expand: Create distinct landing pages per pillar
- Segment ads by avatar: founder/performer, athlete, wellness-focused, productivity-focused
- Test messaging: "Boosted focus" vs "Ritual upgrade" vs "No crash" based on audience

---

### 5. Build Product That's Shareable
**Grüns insight:** Gummies became something people took to social events.
- Unexpected product -> social sharing -> free distribution
- Review language: "shocked", "surprisingly good"
- Became a gift/share item

**Application to Power Coffee:**
- Already an edible product (more shareable than most supplements)
- Create "try Power Coffee" micro-moments (sample packs)
- Social signaling angle: "the coffee a founder uses" or "what Ironman Leo drinks"
- Cape Cod outreach: in-person samples create sharing moment

---

### 6. DTC → Retail Sequencing (Proof First)
**Grüns timeline:**
- Month 0-16: DTC only
- Month 16: Achieved profitability + had proof of demand
- Entered retail with: profitable business, velocity proof, customer data
- Retail velocity: 4.3x category average (entered from position of strength)

**Why this matters:**
- Enters retail conversations from strength (not desperation)
- Has data to support retail claims (not just a pitch)
- Retailers want proven products they can move fast
- Wholesale buyers respect unit economics

**Application to Power Coffee:**
- Currently in retail + DTC mix
- Strengthen DTC profitability signals
- Use 300/70% data + LENS metrics in retail conversations
- Pitch retailers with proof: "Our customers show X attachment rate, Y repeat purchase rate"
- Position as "DTC brand earning retail, not retail brand seeking DTC"

### 7. Build for Exit From Day One
**Grüns founder advantage:**

**Application to Power Coffee:**

- Build metrics acquirers care about:
  - CAC (customer acquisition cost)
  - LTV (lifetime value)
