## Testing Protocol

### Week 1-2: Launch & Learn
- Launch all 4 ad sets with 3 creatives each
- Let them run for 3-4 days with NO changes (let the pixel learn)
- After day 4, check metrics daily

### Kill Criteria (turn off if):
- CPA > $30 after 3 days and 1000+ impressions
- CTR < 0.8% after 1000+ impressions
- No purchases after $20 spent on a single ad

### Scale Criteria (increase budget if):
- CPA < $15 consistently for 3+ days
- ROAS > 2.0x
- CTR > 1.5%

### Week 3-4: Optimize
- Kill bottom 50% of ads
- Create new variations of winning angles
- Test new audiences: Lookalike 1% of purchasers (once you have 25+ purchases)
- Test new creatives from Higgsfield based on winning visual styles
