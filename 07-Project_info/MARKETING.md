## Content Strategy

### Funnel Steps
1. Traffic → Ritual Landing Page
2. Signup → Email Flow (3 emails)
3. Ritual Kit PDF + Follow-up message
4. Feedback Request + Optional Purchase
5. UGC and retention opportunity from test users

### Email Sequences
- Welcome sequence
- Education sequence
- Subscription reminder sequence
- Win-back campaigns
- Health/Beauty benchmark: ~$2.65 revenue per email

### Content Types
- Video testimonials (highest performing)
- UGC from micro-influencers
- Recipe content using Power Coffee
- Ingredient highlight posts
- Founder storytelling
- "Morning Routine" / "What I drink for focus" / "Day in My Life"
- Before/after stories
- Instagram carousels (fatigue education → CTA)

### Influencer Strategy
- Target micro-influencers (5K–50K followers) in health, productivity, fitness
- Provide product, encourage authentic reviews
- Track via affiliate codes
- Creator/influencer affiliate program with tiered rewards

### Blog Ideas
1. How to build a morning routine that lasts
2. The science behind mental fatigue
3. Comparing functional coffees: what matters most
4. How protein and nootropics work together
5. How to turn coffee into a performance drink

### Viral Campaign Idea
- Morning Ritual Challenge: customers post Power Coffee routine with a specific hashtag, weekly winners get free product
