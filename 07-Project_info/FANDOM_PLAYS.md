We're building our first 50 fans, not just buyers. Fans come from real people, real process, generosity and meeting in person. Two plays are active for 90 days:

## Play 1 — Real faces + behind the curtain
- At least 3 of the week's 7 Instagram posts center a REAL person: Leo, his family (with consent), a real customer (with written permission), or a partner café or gym owner.
- Never present an AI-generated person as a customer, user or reviewer. HIGGSFIELD prompts depict product, scenes, hands and objects, or the mascot, never a "customer" face. When a post needs a person, write "REAL PHOTO NEEDED: <what to shoot>" instead of an AI prompt.
- Once a week, a behind-the-curtain post shows how it's made or run: batches, the Illuminate Labs certificate, packing orders, wholesale boxes, a mistake and what changed. Honest, a little unpolished.
- Founder voice is first person and specific: one real moment from this week (training, kids, faith, Cape Cod mornings). Unscripted beats polished. Our best post ever was an unscripted founder Reel.
- Talk with people, not at them. Ask a real question, name a customer (with permission), answer comments.

## Play 2 — In-person tribe on Cape Cod
- A monthly "Power Morning" (Saturday 8–10am at a partner café or gym): buy your latte there, get a free scoop stirred in. Cafés are partners. Never knock regular coffee.
- In the week before an event, at least 2 posts plus the Saturday email invite people to it. In the 24h after, post real event photos (unedited is fine) and tag the venue.
- The event-only code is MORNING15 (live since 2026-10-27: 15% off, one use per customer, one-time purchases).

## CTAs
- At least 2 CTAs a week invite participation instead of a purchase: "send us a photo of your morning cup", "come say hi Saturday", "reply and tell me your 2pm".
- At most one sales CTA per post. Always www.thepowercoffee.com.
