## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

- **Primary:** High-performance adults in the U.S., ages 28–65
- **Roles:** Entrepreneurs, remote workers, executives/consultants, active parents, creatives
- **Psychographics:**
  - Time-starved but health-aware
  - Skeptical of supplement hype
  - Values ingredient transparency and real-world results
  - Digitally native; influenced by TikTok/Instagram/YouTube
- **Key needs:**
  - Sustained energy without anxiety or crash
  - Better focus during demanding days
  - Simple routine; good taste and mixability
  - Easy subscription access
  - Social validation and community

## Persona: Julia

- 38-year-old marketing executive, mom of two
- Remote worker; balances meetings, kids' schedules, and workouts
- Values wellness but no time for complex supplement routines
- **Without Power Coffee:** 2–3 cups of regular coffee daily, midday crashes, trouble sleeping, juggles energy drinks + vitamins + protein powders separately
- **With Power Coffee:** One scoop in morning brew → clean energy and focus through meetings and errands, no crash, no need for second or third cups

## Purchase Triggers

- Feeling overwhelmed or drained midweek
- Recommendation from trusted wellness influencer
- Trying a sample at a coworking event or via TikTok ad

## Purchase Barriers

- Skepticism about supplement claims
- Dislike of overly strong or artificial-tasting coffee
- Fear of wasting money on "another fad product"

## SWOT

**Strengths:**
- Scientifically formulated with functional ingredients
- Sustained focus and energy with no crash/jitters
- Lactose-free, sugar-free, clean-label
- Dual use: standalone or booster
- Strong founder story rooted in real consumer pain
- Early traction through word of mouth

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited brand awareness vs. larger players
- Early-stage production capacity
- Higher price vs. traditional coffee
- Low clinical validation / third-party certification
- Heavily reliant on DTC channels

**Opportunities:**
- Biohacking, wellness, and productivity trends
- Strategic influencer/creator partnerships
- Subscription-based DTC with upsells
- New SKUs: travel sticks, RTD, focus formulas
- Wellness cafés, coworking spaces, startup hubs
- Faith-based market (41M self-identifying faith-driven consumers in U.S.)

**Threats:**
- FDA/FTC regulatory scrutiny on claims
- Well-funded competitor startups
- Commodity price fluctuations (matcha, protein)
- Influencer misinformation risk
- Consumer skepticism from "miracle energy" product saturation

## Strategic Roadmap

### Short-Term (90 Days)
- Launch Starter Kit / Ritual Kit bundling (bag + scoop + 7-day challenge card)
- Aggressive creator testing on Instagram/TikTok to optimize CPA
- Scale DTC + Amazon
- Build email list rapidly

### Medium-Term (6–12 Months)
- Introduce stack bundles (Focus, Metabolic, Pre-Workout) with tiered subscription pricing
- Expand sampling and partnerships (coworking spaces, gyms, boutique cafes)
- Develop stick packs and travel formats
- Test retail entry (Erewhon, Whole Foods Local, boutique fitness cafés)

### Long-Term (18–24 Months)
- Evolve into performance brand with multiple SKUs (RTD, non-caffeinated, recovery blends)
- Digital platform (habit tracker, reorder system, personalized coaching)
- International expansion (Canada, Europe, LATAM)
- Licensing for cafés, coworking spaces, corporate wellness
- Ambassador program for mental performance coaches

## Key External Drivers

1. **Mental health & burnout crisis** — 70% of professionals report daily fatigue; $61B workplace wellness industry
2. **Biohacking & functional foods growth** — 2 in 5 millennials use nootropics weekly; functional coffee searches up 45% YoY
3. **Decline of traditional energy drinks** — 58% of Gen Z prefer plant-based alternatives
4. **Remote & hybrid work** — 58% of remote workers seek better focus solutions
5. **Influencer-led wellness** — 65% of supplement buyers influenced by social proof
6. **Regulatory scrutiny** — FDA tightening on supplement labeling; opportunity for transparent brands
