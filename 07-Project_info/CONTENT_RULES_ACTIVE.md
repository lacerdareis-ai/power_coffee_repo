# Active content rules from Leo (set 2026-11-23). These override pillar rotation and templates.

## 1. Lead with the client's problem (all of week Nov 23-29, 2026, and by default after)
- Every post starts from a real problem the customer lives with: the post-lunch fog, the plan that dies by 11am,
  rereading the same email, the second cup at 3pm that doesn't work, coming home with nothing left for family.
- The Power Coffee's purpose is the answer: help people have better days, with more focus and energy for what matters.
- Do NOT lead with the product, ingredients, milligrams or features. Ingredients may appear at most once, low in the
  caption, never on slide 1 and never as the headline. Testimonials, founder stories and offers are framed as proof
  or answers to the problem, not as the topic.

## 2. A trigger on slide 1 and on every Story's first frame
- Slide 1 / Story frame 1 must stop the scroll in under 2 seconds with an attention trigger, for example:
  - a call-out of a moment they know ("2:15pm. Third time reading the same email.")
  - a question that names their pain ("Why is your best energy gone by lunch?")
  - a contrarian line ("Your second coffee isn't helping.")
  - a specific number or time stamp, or "If you..." direct address.
- Short: 4-9 words, big type. No brand name or product as the hook.

## 3. Always two CTAs: the website AND Amazon
- Every carousel's last slide, every Story's last frame, every caption, X post/thread end, LinkedIn post and
  WhatsApp message ends with both:
  - Website: thepowercoffee.com (landing page: thepowercoffee.com/pages/betterday)
  - Amazon: amazon.com/dp/B0DYK75WP7 (on images, write "Also on Amazon")
- When a discount code is in play, say the code works on the website only.
