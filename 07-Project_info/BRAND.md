# Brand — The Power Coffee

## ⭐ CONTENT NORTH STAR (mandatory — applies to every piece of content)

**Lead with the PERSONA and THEIR PROBLEM, never the product or its ingredients.** Customers do not care how many ingredients it has, the mg of caffeine, or the ingredient science. They care about solving their **energy and focus problems** — the 2pm crash, the third cup that isn't working, the afternoon fog, the day that falls apart by mid-afternoon.

- **Hook** on the persona's real moment: the pain (drained, foggy, wired-but-tired) or the solved outcome (steady energy, real focus, a day that holds).
- **Ingredients/specs are a receipt, not the pitch.** They may appear later as quiet supporting proof — NEVER as the headline, the opening line, or the point of the post.
- Talk about the customer and their life, not about the brand and its formula.
- **Visuals:** show the persona living the problem or the solved outcome (focused, energized, in flow) — not the product as hero, and never brewed coffee/mugs/beans (Power Coffee is a supplement/booster added to coffee, not coffee itself).

This overrides any older section below that leads with product features or ingredient lists.

## Compliance — FDA/FTC Guardrails

### Never say:
- Disease claims or treatment language ("treats anxiety," "cures fatigue," "prevents Alzheimer's")
- Guarantees ("will," "always," "100%")
- Medical advice or personalized diagnosis
- Misleading comparisons or unsubstantiated superiority

### Safe language:
- "supports," "helps," "designed to," "may," "associated with," "can be part of"
- Emphasize individual variability ("results vary," "some people…")

### Required disclaimers (when relevant):
- "Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- "Not recommended for pregnant or breastfeeding individuals."
- Encourage consulting a healthcare professional when appropriate.

## Messaging Rules

1. Start with the user's outcome (what they want)
2. Give a direct answer in 1–3 sentences
3. Provide actionable steps or options
4. End with a clear next step or CTA

## Competitive Mentions

When comparing to competitors (Mud/Wtr, Everyday Dose, Thesis, Magic Mind, etc.):
- Compare positioning and use-case (ritual compatibility, focus + performance stack, simplicity)
- Avoid defamatory claims; keep neutral and evidence-based

## Differentiation Thesis

- Fits into existing coffee routine (booster vs. replacement)
- Only functional coffee combining protein + nootropics + clean energy
- Clean-label, sugar-free, lactose-free
- Faith-driven / purpose-driven brand story — no competitor touches this
- Familiar coffee ritual = lower adoption friction
