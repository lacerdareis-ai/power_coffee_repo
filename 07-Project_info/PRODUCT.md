## Usage Guidance

- **Standard:** One scoop (15g) in 8–10 oz hot water, cold water, or plant-based milk
- **Booster mode:** Add one scoop to your regular coffee
- **Timing:** Morning or mid-morning. Avoid late-day use for sleep-sensitive customers.
- **Sensitivity:** Some users prefer half dose. Anxiety-sensitive customers may want lower dose.
- **Not recommended for:** Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals
