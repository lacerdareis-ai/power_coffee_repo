# Voice of customer: the afternoon slump (active from 2026-11-09)

Loaded by generate_content.py. Full notes: PC Brain "VOC — Afternoon Slump" (Insightios report, 1,589 Reddit comments + US search data).

How real people talk about our moment, and how to write for it:

## What they already believe
- The slump comes from LUNCH ("carb coma"). Biggest theme by far. Say "the post-lunch slump" / "after lunch", not only "2pm".
- Their first fixes are free: a walk after lunch, water, a nap. Work WITH these ("lunch, a short walk, and the coffee you already had"), never against them.
- The obvious fix is a second coffee at 3pm, and many say it backfires: it doesn't fix the crash or it costs them sleep.
- Their word for a better option is "smoother" (vs "jittery").

## Lines that come from their words (use these angles)
- "Keep your morning coffee. Skip the 3pm one."
- "For the post-lunch slump, without a second cup."
- "Without stacking another cup."

## Words
- USE: slump, crash, after lunch, 2pm, second cup, jittery, smoother.
- USE LESS: "booster" as the hook or headline. Nobody says it. Fine lower in the copy.
- SEO: "coffee crash", "afternoon slump", "afternoon coffee", "functional coffee" for blog titles and alt text.

## Rules (compliance)
- 175 mg caffeine per serving. NEVER say or imply it helps sleep, "won't keep you up", or "no crash". The honest claim is one morning cup instead of adding a 3pm cup.
- Softeners on outcomes: "designed to carry you through the afternoon", "supports focus". Never "fixes/ends the slump". FDA disclaimer in the caption.
- Never knock coffee, naps, matcha or competitors. Contrast the ritual (one cup, not two).
- The Reddit comments are research. Never quote them on images or present them as customers or reviews.
- Don't target people quitting coffee (unknown reaction).

## Frequency
Through Dec 2026: at least 2 posts a week use the post-lunch slump angle or its language (Problem Hook and CTA pillars fit best).
