# Different Markets, Same Deliverable: Growth and Sales

How I create real impact for companies of every size, across different markets.

Three years ago I started asking two questions before taking on any project: what happens to this once I leave the room? And how do I build marketing and sales engines that actually work?

Those questions changed the kind of work I do. Here is what came out of it.

**An insurance agency needed more than a new logo**
I redesigned the marketing process and built the international team to carry it. The growth followed because the process itself was built right.

**Thirty entrepreneurs, one church, the same result**
Through an entrepreneur ministry inside my church, I've worked with 30 entrepreneurs on growing their businesses. Nothing different. Just the same systems thinking, applied where it's needed most.

**An asphalt company that didn't exist a year ago**
I built one from the ground up. Hundreds of customers in the first six months, not from luck, from a repeatable acquisition and sales process built before the first truck rolled out.

**A pool company that needed structure, not just ads**
Full marketing and sales strategy, then a project management process in Monday.com, so the team could actually run what we designed after I stepped back.

**A marketing playbook a major innovation consulting firm now hands to every client**
Not a one-off campaign. A repeatable system their own team runs, project after project.

**HE-OS: an AI agent that doesn't wait for me**
For my own coffee brand, I built an autonomous system that watches competitors and trends, writes and publishes content, and manages sales across Shopify and Amazon. It runs daily without me touching it.

**A high-end construction company, five brands, three continents**
Brazil, the US, Europe, under one umbrella. I built the marketing engine that lets five brands move as one company instead of five disconnected teams.

Every one of these companies will tell you the same thing: more awareness, stronger brand position, new client acquisition, and sales, millions of dollars of it.

This was never about the money. It's about the passion for building precise systems, marketing and sales working as one, teams, tools and technology aligned around a single focus: RESULTS.
