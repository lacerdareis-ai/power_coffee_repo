# Most high performers optimize their schedule but neglect the engine that runs it.

## Post Content

Most high performers optimize their schedule but neglect the engine that runs it.

Your brain is the most energy-hungry organ in your body.

And you're feeding it on autopilot.

Here's what I've learned working with top-performing teams and founders: **energy is a strategy, not a luxury.**

When your blood sugar crashes at 2pm, you don't just feel tired — you make worse decisions, lose focus faster, and communicate with less clarity.

That's not a time management problem. That's a fuel problem.

**3 nutrition principles that changed how I operate:**

**1. Front-load your protein.**
Eating 30–40g of protein at breakfast stabilizes blood sugar for hours. Less spiking. Less crashing. Sharper mornings.

**2. Treat your biggest meal as a reward, not a fuel source.**
Most people eat their heaviest meal at midday. Then wonder why their afternoon is slow. Lighter lunches with quality fats and complex carbs keep you in flow longer.

**3. Hydration before caffeine.**
16oz of water before your first coffee. Always. Dehydration creates mental fog before you even realize it. Caffeine on top of that just masks the problem.

These aren't hacks. They're fundamentals.

The highest-output leaders I know treat food the same way they treat their calendar — intentionally.

They know that what they eat at 7am shapes the quality of the decision they make at 3pm.

You can't optimize performance from an empty or misfueled tank.

Start treating nutrition like the competitive advantage it actually is.
