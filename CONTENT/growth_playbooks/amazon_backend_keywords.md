**Purpose:** Organic ranking + discovery | **Location:** Amazon backend search terms field | **Character limit:** 250 bytes

---

## What Are Backend Keywords?

These are **hidden keywords** you add to your Amazon seller dashboard that don't appear in the listing, but help Amazon's algorithm match your product to customer searches.

**Where to add:** Seller Central → Inventory → Your Products → Edit Product → Search Terms field

## How Backend Keywords Differ from Title/Bullets

| Element | Location | Purpose | Include Competitor Names? |
|---------|----------|---------|--------------------------|
| **Title** | Front & center | Customer sees first | ❌ No (use brand + main benefit) |
| **Bullets** | First 5 lines | Highlight benefits | ❌ No (focus on features) |
| **Backend Keywords** | Hidden | Amazon algorithm | ✅ Yes (helps discovery) |
| **Description** | Below bullets | Full story | ❌ No (save for copy) |

## How Amazon Uses Backend Keywords

1. **Customer searches "no crash coffee"** → Amazon checks backend keywords
2. **Match found** → Your listing becomes eligible to rank
3. **More relevance signals** (reviews, price, conversion rate) → Ranking improves
4. **Organic traffic increases** → More sales, better ranking (flywheel)

---

## Amazon Backend Keywords Best Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Include plural + singular forms (coffee, coffees)
- Use natural language (how people actually search)
- Include common misspellings (adaptagen instead of adaptogen)
- Add athlete/performance keywords (fitness audience high-intent)
- Use spaces as separators (not commas for phrases)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Stuff competitor brand names (Amazon penalizes)
- Repeat keywords (wastes character limit)
- Use all caps
- Use special characters
- Include your brand name (already in title)

3. Analyze:

   - Which drive conversions?

4. Add high-converting search terms back into backend keywords
