## 0. Meta compliance rules baked into this copy (read first)
Meta rejects supplement ads that **assert or imply a personal attribute** or a **health outcome**. That changes how we write the hook:
- ❌ Not allowed: "Still crashing by 2pm?", "You're exhausted", "Fix your fatigue." (asserts the reader's state/condition)
- ✅ Allowed: "The 2pm crash is real.", "I used to hit a wall every afternoon.", "That third-coffee slump everyone talks about." (first-person or general observation)
- ❌ No "boosts energy / cures / treats / guaranteed results" as a medical outcome. ✅ "clean energy, no jitters" as an experience, plus factual certification.
- Keep the certification factual ("independently certified, reviewed by an MD"), never as an efficacy guarantee.
- No unverified specifics in copy: no protein grams, no mg counts, no "vanilla" (per the cert file discrepancies).
