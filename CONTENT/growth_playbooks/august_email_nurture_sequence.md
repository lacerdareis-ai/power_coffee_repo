## Rules baked into these emails
- **List:** send ONLY to people who downloaded the ebook (a real, permissioned opt-in list). Do NOT merge this into the 8,000 Gmail-contacts file. That file is a liability, not a list.
- **Voice:** founder-personal, first person, from Leo. Emails 1 and 2 should look almost plain-text (like a real person wrote them). Emails 3 to 5 can carry the branded HTML template with a product image and a button.
- **Persona-first:** every email opens with her problem (the 2pm crash, being burned by supplements before), not the ingredients. Ingredients are a receipt, never the pitch.
- **Honest only:** no invented testimonials or stats. Where a real customer quote should go, there is a `[[PLACEHOLDER]]` to drop in a genuine review. Do not fabricate one.
- **No unverified specifics:** no protein grams, no mg counts, no "vanilla" (per the cert file).
- **CAN-SPAM:** every email needs a working unsubscribe link and a physical mailing address in the footer.
- **Attribution:** all buy links use `?utm_source=email&utm_medium=nurture&utm_campaign=aug_ebook&utm_content=email_N` and the code **`AUGEMAIL`**.
- **Offer values to confirm with Leo:** the welcome discount amount (placeholder `[WELCOME OFFER]`, e.g. 15% off first order or free shipping) and the real deadline for it. Everything else (prices $18.90 / $49.90, 30-day guarantee, certification, 4.9 stars from 112 verified buyers) is set.
