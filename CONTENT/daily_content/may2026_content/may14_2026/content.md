### Body

## The 2pm Crash Isn't a You Problem

If you've hit a wall at 2pm more times than you can count, you're not alone — and you're not broken. The afternoon energy dip is a physiological pattern. A combination of circadian rhythm shifts, post-lunch glucose fluctuation, and the tail-end of a typical caffeine half-life conspires to flatten most people right in the middle of their most important hours.

Energy drinks promise to fix it. For a lot of people, they just move the problem.

## What Most Energy Products Get Wrong

The core issue is simple: isolated, high-dose caffeine spikes blood levels fast and drops them just as fast. You get the lift, then you get the crash. Add in artificial colors, synthetic B-vitamins, and 50 grams of sugar, and you've also stacked blood sugar volatility on top of the caffeine curve.

The people who feel worst after energy drinks aren't sensitive to caffeine — they're experiencing exactly what the formula produces.

## A Different Approach: Stack the Ingredients, Smooth the Curve

Power Coffee was built around a different model. Instead of maximizing the peak, the goal is extending the plateau.

Here's how:

**L-theanine + caffeine** is one of the most well-researched combinations in cognitive performance. L-theanine blunts the jittery edge of caffeine without reducing alertness. The result is calm focus rather than wired urgency.

**Matcha** contributes catechins that extend caffeine's effective window by slowing how quickly it's metabolized. A smaller amount of caffeine with matcha tends to outlast a larger dose without it.

**Pea protein** slows gastric emptying, which smooths absorption of every ingredient in the cup — flattening spikes and extending the effect curve.

**Taurine** at 2g supports mitochondrial function and neurotransmitter balance, which is why it appears in nearly every energy product on the market — just rarely at therapeutic levels.

## What the Switch Actually Feels Like

We hear a version of the same story repeatedly. The first week, people notice they're not crashing. The second week, they notice they're more consistent. By the third week, the old pattern — spike, drop, drag — is just a memory.

It's not dramatic. It doesn't feel like a biohack. It feels like finally having a tool that matches the job.
