## The Feedback That Matters Most

When you build a product to solve a real problem, the feedback that means most isn't the enthusiastic first impression — it's what people say after a week of normal days. After the 12-hour shifts and the 6am alarms and the back-to-back afternoon meetings.

That's where the proof lives.

Here's what The Power Coffee community keeps telling us — in DMs, in reviews, in messages Leo reads every morning.

## "I Stopped Thinking About My Second Coffee"

One of the most common patterns: people stop reaching for a second coffee mid-morning and don't realize it for several days. When they notice, they trace it back to the same thing — the first cup doesn't drop off the way they're used to.

That's not an accident. The formula includes matcha for slow-release catechins, which extend the energy window past the acute caffeine phase. L-theanine smooths the onset curve. The result is a longer, flatter energy profile instead of the spike-and-drop most caffeine delivery produces.

## The Nurse on 12-Hour Shifts

"I was skeptical of anything called functional. But I'm a nurse working 12-hour shifts and I genuinely cannot afford to crash. This is the first thing that's worked without making me feel wired and weird."

That description — "worked without feeling wired and weird" — points directly to the taurine and L-theanine combination in the formula. Taurine (2g) regulates the central nervous system response, keeping stimulation functional rather than anxious. The goal was never peak stimulation. It was sustained, usable clarity.

## The Doubter Who Reorders

"My husband tried it to prove a point and now he asks me to reorder. He'll never admit it."

Skepticism is a reasonable response to any functional supplement claim. The formula was designed to be tested exactly that way — not to impress on first use, but to produce a measurable difference in how a week feels. When someone sets out to disprove it and quietly becomes a customer, that's the kind of proof that isn't staged.

## What These Stories Have in Common

Nobody leads with taste. They lead with what changed.

The 2pm crash that didn't come. The second coffee they stopped needing. The afternoon meeting they actually showed up to with full capacity.

That's what 11 ingredients, each selected for a specific mechanism, is designed to produce. Not a label claim — a different day.
