## What's In The Power Coffee

Eleven ingredients. Each one chosen for a reason. Here's the short version:

**Taurine (2g):** One of the most under-discussed performance ingredients. Taurine supports cardiovascular function, reduces oxidative stress, and has been studied for its role in reducing exercise-induced fatigue. At 2g, it's at or above the doses used in most clinical research.

**L-Theanine + Caffeine (~175mg):** The pairing isn't new, but it matters. L-theanine blunts the cortisol spike that comes with caffeine alone, extending focus and reducing the anxious edge most coffee drinkers know well. The result is calm, sustained alertness — not a jolt.

**Matcha:** Provides a slower caffeine release curve compared to coffee alone, adding to the smoothness of the overall energy profile.

**Pea Protein (5g):** Keeps blood sugar steadier through the morning, which is directly related to how long the energy holds. Crash? Usually a blood sugar story.

**Ginkgo Biloba:** Used in traditional medicine and studied in modern research for its role in cerebral circulation and cognitive performance.

**Cinnamon:** More than a flavor note. Cinnamon has a documented effect on blood sugar regulation — another lever on that afternoon crash.
