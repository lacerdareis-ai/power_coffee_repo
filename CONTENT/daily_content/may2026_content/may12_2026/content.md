### Title
Taurine at 2 Grams: Why Dose Is the Difference Between a Label and a Function

### Body
## What Taurine Actually Does

If you've seen taurine on an energy drink can and assumed it was filler, you're not wrong — at the doses most brands use, it basically is.

But taurine at a clinical 2-gram dose is a different story. And it's one of the most important ingredients in The Power Coffee for a reason that has nothing to do with stimulation.

Taurine is a conditionally essential amino acid (technically a sulfonic acid) found in high concentrations in the heart, brain, and skeletal muscle. The body produces it, but not always at the levels needed for sustained high-output performance — especially under stress.

## The Three Mechanisms That Matter

**1. Cardiovascular efficiency.** Research at the 2g dose shows that taurine supports cardiac muscle contraction and blood pressure regulation. The result: your heart moves blood more efficiently under load. This isn't about increasing heart rate — it's about improving the quality of each beat.

**2. Neuromuscular endurance.** Taurine has been shown to reduce markers of muscle fatigue and oxidative damage during sustained effort. For knowledge workers and athletes alike, this translates to a longer window before physical and cognitive output degrades.

**3. Neuroprotection.** Taurine acts as an inhibitory neurotransmitter and antioxidant in the brain. Studies suggest it helps protect neurons from oxidative stress — creating a more stable cognitive environment for the work that follows your morning routine.

## Why Dose Is Everything

The average energy drink contains 500mg to 1g of taurine. The studies showing measurable benefits were conducted at 2 grams. This isn't a minor difference — it's the gap between a compound that appears on a label and one that actually does something.

We use 2g per serving in The Power Coffee. Not because it sounds better in marketing copy, but because 2g is what the research used.

## How It Fits the Stack

Taurine doesn't replace caffeine. It extends it. Caffeine blocks adenosine and creates the alertness window. L-theanine shapes the curve — smoother onset, longer plateau. Taurine holds the floor — the cardiovascular and cognitive infrastructure that keeps you operating past the point where caffeine alone would have dropped.

Together, these three compounds form the core mechanism of why The Power Coffee lasts through the morning in a way that single-ingredient coffee simply can't.

## The Takeaway

Read labels. Ask about doses. An ingredient at half the clinical amount is not the same as a functional ingredient. Your morning stack should be built around what actually works — not what looks good on the can.
