### Body
## What Is Ginkgo Biloba?

Ginkgo biloba is one of the oldest living tree species on Earth — and one of the most studied botanicals in modern science. With over 400 published clinical trials, it sits at a rare intersection: centuries of traditional use confirmed by rigorous research.

Despite its credentials, almost no functional coffee brand has included it. That's the gap Power Coffee was built to close.

## How It Works

The active compounds in ginkgo — ginkgolides and bilobalides — operate through two primary pathways.

Ginkgolides improve cerebrovascular circulation by inhibiting platelet-activating factor, which dilates blood vessels in the brain and increases blood flow. More blood flow means more oxygen and glucose reaching the neurons that drive attention, memory, and executive function.

Bilobalides act as neuroprotectants, reducing oxidative stress in neural tissue. This means ginkgo isn't only supporting cognitive performance today — research suggests it may help protect long-term brain health as well.

## What the Research Shows

A 2016 Cochrane Review analyzed 21 randomized controlled trials and found consistent associations between ginkgo supplementation and improvements in attention, memory, and processing speed in healthy adults.

A study published in *Psychopharmacology* found that a ginkgo and ginseng combination improved working memory performance by 7.5% versus placebo. The mechanisms are understood. The data is reproducible.

## Why Pair It with Caffeine?

Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors and temporarily increases alertness — but stimulation isn't the same as focus. Your brain still needs adequate blood flow and oxygen delivery to convert that alertness into sustained cognitive output.

Ginkgo fills that gap. Caffeine turns up the signal. Ginkgo improves the channel it travels through.

Together, they address two distinct but complementary levers on morning performance: energy and cerebral blood supply.

function addRect(parent, x, y, w, h, color, opacity = 1) {
  const r = figma.createRectangle();
  r.x = x; r.y = y; r.resize(w, h);
  r.fills = [{ type: "SOLID", color: col(color), opacity }];
  parent.appendChild(r);
  return r;
}

async function addText(parent, content, x, y, w, size, weight, color, align = "LEFT", lineH = 1.05) {
  await figma.loadFontAsync({ family: "Inter", style: weight });
  const t = figma.createText();
  t.fontName = { family: "Inter", style: weight };
  t.characters = content;
  t.fontSize = size;
  t.textAlignHorizontal = align;
  t.fills = [{ type: "SOLID", color: col(color) }];
  t.lineHeight = { unit: "PERCENT", value: lineH * 100 };
  t.x = x; t.y = y;
  t.resize(w, t.height);
  parent.appendChild(t);
  return t;
}
