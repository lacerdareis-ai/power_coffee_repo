## SHOPIFY BLOG

### Title
Why Your 10am Crash Has Nothing to Do With Caffeine

### Body
Three coffees in and still foggy at 10am. Most people blame caffeine tolerance. The real explanation is more useful — and more fixable.

Caffeine has a half-life of approximately five hours. When you drink your first cup at 7am, a significant portion of it is still active in your system by 10am. So when the crash arrives mid-morning, caffeine levels are rarely the culprit.

What crashes at 10am is your blood sugar.

**The two-crash mechanism**

Caffeine stimulates the release of cortisol and adrenaline, which triggers a temporary spike in blood glucose. When that spike resolves — usually 2 to 3 hours after your first cup — blood sugar drops. For most people, that drop arrives somewhere between 9:30 and 11am, depending on what (or whether) they ate.

The result is familiar: a heaviness behind the eyes, a slight difficulty focusing, an impulse to reach for the next cup. But that second cup adds more cortisol on top of a blood sugar dip. It can close the gap temporarily, but it doesn't resolve the underlying instability.

**Why cinnamon is a functional ingredient, not a flavoring**

Research indicates that cinnamon may support blood glucose stability, particularly after meals and stimulant consumption. The mechanism involves improving insulin sensitivity and slowing the post-stimulus glucose drop. When blood sugar levels hold steadier, the mid-morning cognitive dip arrives later and with less severity.

This is why Power Coffee includes cinnamon in every serving — not for flavor, but because blood sugar stability is directly tied to focus quality across a full morning.

**What stable energy actually feels like**

The goal is not a higher peak. Most people don't need more stimulation. They need the floor to hold longer. A morning where energy levels don't require active management — no third cup, no afternoon scramble, no dependence on glucose from a snack to get through a meeting.

The Power Coffee formula addresses both sides of the crash: the caffeine arc (matcha and L-theanine smooth the spike and extend the hold) and the blood sugar arc (pea protein and cinnamon reduce the drop). Together, they shift the experience from managing energy to simply having it.

One scoop in your existing coffee. No new ritual required.
