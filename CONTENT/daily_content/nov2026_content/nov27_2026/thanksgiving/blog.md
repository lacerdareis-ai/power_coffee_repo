## Four small ways to have a better day after the holiday

The days after Thanksgiving can feel heavy. A few simple habits help:

1. **Get outside early.** Ten minutes of morning daylight helps set the tone for the day.
2. **Drink water before the second cup.** A glass of water first is an easy win after a big meal.
3. **Plan the afternoon in the morning.** Put your hardest task before lunch, while your focus is fresh.
4. **Make the first cup count.** One scoop of The Power Coffee in the coffee you already drink is designed to support steady energy and focus into the afternoon.
