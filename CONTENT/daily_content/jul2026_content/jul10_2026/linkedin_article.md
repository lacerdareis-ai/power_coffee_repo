## ARTICLE

I want to tell you something most founders don't say out loud.

I woke up this morning and checked my Shopify dashboard. Zero orders. I checked Amazon. Zero orders. The same as yesterday.

This is day 10 of July. I set a public goal: sell 100 packs of Power Coffee this month. I have a buy-2-get-1-free offer running. I sent 7,578 emails four days ago. I have 28,000 followers on Instagram. I post every day. I built an automated content and email system from scratch that runs without me.

Zero orders.

I'm not writing this for sympathy. I'm writing it because I think a lot of people in my network have seen me post about The Power Coffee for months — the formula, the mornings, the numbers — and haven't done anything. Including me asking them to.

So let me be specific today.

**What I built**

Power Coffee is a functional supplement you add to the coffee you already make. One scoop. 11 ingredients: protein, taurine, matcha, L-theanine, ginkgo biloba. 175mg clean caffeine. No artificial sweeteners. No flavor masking. The first buyer review called it "rich, smooth, and perfectly flavored." She was surprised. Most people are.

I've used it every morning for 620 days. I built the formula because I needed it and couldn't find it anywhere else.

It sells for $18.90 a bag. 15 servings. $1.26 a morning.

**What isn't working**

I know how to build things. I know how to write. I know how to automate. I know how to show up consistently.

What I'm learning — the hard way, in real time, this week — is that none of that closes the gap between someone knowing a product exists and actually buying it.

Distribution is the problem. Not the product. Not the price. Not the brand. The problem is that I'm talking to the same 28,000 people every day, and most of them are not the person who needs this product right now.

**What I'm asking**

I'm not asking you to buy it. I mean, you can — and if you've been thinking about it, now is a good time, there's a code at the bottom of this post.

But what I'm actually asking is this:

Do you know someone who would drink this?

The person who has two cups of coffee before 9am and still feels tired by noon. The person who gave up energy drinks but hasn't found a replacement that doesn't crash them. The person who works hard, shows up for everything, and is quietly running on empty.

If that person exists in your network — a text, a DM, a mention in the comments, forwarding this article — that's what moves the needle for a brand at my stage. Not ads. Not algorithms. A person you trust saying "try this."

I built a real product. I tested it for almost two years. The people who try it come back. The problem is getting it to the first person in a new circle.

That's what I need from you today. A name. A share. A forward.

**The offer, since you're here**

If you want to try it yourself: buy 2 bags, get a third free. Code FOURTH at thepowercoffee.com. Expires July 31.

Three bags. 45 mornings. $37.80. $1.26 a day.

If it doesn't change your mornings, I want to hear about it.
