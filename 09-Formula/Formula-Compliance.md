# Power Coffee — Formula Compliance Guide
_FTC / FDA rules for ingredient claims_

> Cross-reference: [[Formula-Master]], [[Formula-Research]]

---

## The Core Rule

Power Coffee is a **dietary supplement / functional food** sold in the US. Claims are governed by:
- **FDA:** Structure/function claims only — no disease claims
- **FTC:** Truth in advertising — claims must be substantiated

**Structure/function claim (✅ allowed):**
> "Supports mental clarity and sustained energy"

**Disease claim (❌ not allowed):**
> "Treats ADHD" / "Prevents diabetes" / "Cures fatigue"

---

## Safe Language — Use These

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| "proven to" | "research suggests" / "studies indicate" |
| "clinically proven" | "backed by research" / "studied for" |
| "boosts" | "supports" / "helps maintain" / "associated with" |
| "eliminates crashes" | "designed to support consistent energy" |
| "treats fatigue" | "may help reduce tiredness" |
| "fixes blood sugar" | "may support blood sugar stability" |
| "burns fat" | "may support fat metabolism" |
| "improves memory" | "associated with cognitive support" |

## Per-Ingredient Compliant Framing

### Caffeine
✅ "Supports alertness and focus"
✅ "Caffeine is the most studied cognitive stimulant — research associates it with improved reaction time and attention"
❌ "Cures brain fog" / "Eliminates fatigue permanently"

### L-Theanine
✅ "Studies suggest L-theanine combined with caffeine may support sustained alertness with fewer side effects than caffeine alone"
✅ "May help maintain calm focus"
❌ "Eliminates anxiety" / "Treats anxiety disorders"

### Taurine
✅ "Research suggests taurine supports neurological balance, which may contribute to focus without the jittery edge associated with caffeine-only formulas"
✅ "May help maintain neurological balance"
❌ "Boosts brain power" / "Treats overstimulation"

### Pea Protein
✅ "Research indicates blood sugar stability is associated with more consistent cognitive performance"
✅ "May help support blood sugar stability after stimulant consumption"
❌ "Prevents blood sugar crashes" / "Treats hypoglycemia"

### Cinnamon
✅ "Research indicates cinnamon may support blood sugar stability by improving insulin sensitivity"
✅ "May support consistent energy levels through the morning"
❌ "Treats diabetes" / "Controls blood sugar" / "Prevents insulin resistance"

### Ginkgo Biloba
✅ "Research associates ginkgo biloba extract with improved blood flow to the brain and may support cognitive endurance"
✅ "May support blood circulation to the brain"
❌ "Treats Alzheimer's" / "Prevents dementia" / "Cures memory loss"

### L-Carnitine
✅ "May support the body's use of fat as fuel and has been studied for its role in supporting mental energy"
✅ "May support fat metabolism and energy production"
❌ "Burns fat" / "Causes weight loss" / "Treats fatigue disorder"

## Required Disclaimer

Add this to every product page, ingredient transparency section, and any communication making structure/function claims:

> *"These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."*

---

## Dose Claims — What's Allowed

Listing specific doses (e.g., "2,000mg Taurine per serving") is allowed and encouraged — it's a structure/function transparency claim, not a medical claim. Stating a dose does not constitute a disease claim.

**Safe dose claim format:**
> "2,000mg Taurine — formulated to support neurological balance and reduce overstimulation from caffeine"

**Avoid pairing dose with disease:**
> ~~"2,000mg Taurine — clinically proven to prevent caffeine anxiety"~~

## Certifications to Consider (future)

| Certification | What it signals | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| NSF Certified for Sport | Third-party tested, no banned substances — good for athlete audience | Medium |
| Informed-Sport | Same as NSF, UK origin, widely recognized | Medium |
| Non-GMO Project Verified | Clean label trust signal | Low-Medium |
| USDA Organic (if applicable) | Premium positioning | Low (cost-intensive) |
| COA (Certificate of Analysis) | Per-batch lab results — post on site, available on request | **High — do this now** |

**Immediate action:** Get a Certificate of Analysis (COA) from your manufacturer for each batch. Post it on the website or make it downloadable. This is the fastest way to signal the transparency the brand promises.
