## Key Differentiators

**What only Power Coffee has:**
1. **Taurine at meaningful dose (2,000mg)** — no functional coffee competitor includes taurine, let alone at 2g
2. **Pea protein (5g)** — no functional coffee competitor includes protein for blood sugar management
3. **Both caffeine sources (coffee + matcha)** — dual caffeine delivery for smoother curve
4. **Blood sugar protocol (protein + cinnamon)** — the only brand explicitly addressing the glucose crash mechanism

**What competitors have that Power Coffee doesn't:**
- Mushrooms (lion's mane, reishi, chaga) — Power Coffee explicitly avoids the "quit coffee" mushroom positioning
- Collagen (Everyday Dose) — adds gut/skin benefit layer
- Higher serving count per pack — most competitors are 30 servings; PC is 15

## Positioning Gaps to Exploit

### vs Everyday Dose ($1.30/serving)
Their weakness: mushroom + collagen formula is "functional" but doesn't address the crash mechanism (no taurine, no protein, no blood sugar support). Their "gut health" angle is soft — hard to feel in daily use.
Our angle: "They have mushrooms. We have 2g taurine and 5g protein. Which one actually changes how you feel at 10am?"

### vs Ryze ($1.20/serving)
Their weakness: mushroom positioning is polarizing ("quit coffee" framing) + no protein + no taurine.
Our angle: "We didn't ask you to give up coffee. We upgraded it. Same ritual — better formula."

### vs Mud/Wtr ($1.33/serving)
Their weakness: no real caffeine (cacao only = ~25mg). Their customer is quitting coffee, not upgrading it. Completely different audience.
Our angle: Different customer. Not a competitor for the buyer who wants coffee to work better.

### vs Four Sigmatic (~$1.17/serving)
Their weakness: High brand recognition but complex mushroom chemistry that's hard to verify. Expensive for what it is. No protein, no taurine.
Our angle: "We're cheaper, we explain every dose, and we address the actual crash mechanism."
