## Hook Performance Data (Sales Motor May 4–22, 2026)

| Ingredient | Hook Style | ER | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ginkgo Biloba | "The tree that survived a nuclear blast" | **10.53%** | Best in period. Historical claim → curiosity → ingredient reveal |
| Pea Protein | "THERE'S PROTEIN IN YOUR COFFEE" | **2.22%** | Worst. Direct ingredient name kills intrigue |

**The rule:** Never lead with the ingredient name. Lead with the mechanism, the history, or the pain it solves. The ingredient is the payoff, not the hook.

## Per-Ingredient Content Playbook

### Instant Coffee + Caffeine
**Don't say:** "We use real coffee for energy"
**Do say:** "Your crash at 10am isn't caffeine wearing off. Caffeine's half-life is 5 hours — it's still in your system. What drops is your blood sugar."
**Best platform:** X (contrarian mechanism post), LinkedIn (science explainer)
**Angle that works:** Reframe the problem. The buyer thinks caffeine is failing them. Show them it's blood sugar — and that Power Coffee addresses both.

---

### Matcha
**Don't say:** "Contains matcha for sustained energy"
**Do say:** "Matcha's caffeine doesn't spike and crash. It's bound to L-theanine in the leaf. That's why the energy holds 4–6 hours instead of 45 minutes."
**Best platform:** Instagram Carousel (ingredient science, P2 pillar), X thread
**Angle that works:** The mechanism — why matcha *feels* different from coffee. The audience already knows matcha is "healthy." Explain why.

---

### L-Theanine
**Don't say:** "Contains L-theanine for calm focus"
**Do say:** "The reason you feel anxious after coffee but not after matcha? L-theanine. We add 200mg separately so the dose is consistent, not batch-dependent."
**Best platform:** Instagram (P2 science), email
**Angle that works:** The "why matcha feels different" hook opens it; L-theanine is the answer. Never lead with "L-theanine" as a word — nobody knows what it is until you explain the effect first.

---

### Taurine ⭐ (highest-leverage underused angle)
**Don't say:** "Taurine for performance"
**Do say:** "Taurine is in Red Bull — but not for the reason you think. It doesn't stimulate. It stops stimulants from going too far. We put in 2,000mg. That's double what's in a Red Bull."
**Best platform:** X (contrarian), Instagram Reel hook, LinkedIn
**Angle that works:** The Red Bull comparison disarms skepticism (familiar reference), then flips it (we use it correctly). The 2,000mg vs 1,000mg comparison is a concrete differentiator. This angle is almost entirely unused in current content — high opportunity.
**FTC note:** "may support neurological balance" / "associated with focus without jitteriness"

---

### Pea Protein ⭐ (most misunderstood ingredient)
**Don't say:** "5g of plant protein" or "THERE'S PROTEIN IN YOUR COFFEE"
**Do say:** "5 grams of protein per scoop. Not for muscle — for blood sugar. Protein slows the glucose drop that causes your 10am crash. Most functional coffees don't do this."
**Best platform:** Instagram Carousel (P2), email
**Angle that works:** Reframe protein as functional, not nutritional. Buyers associate "protein in coffee" with a weird gym product. The blood sugar mechanism is the explanation that makes it click.
**Performance note:** The ingredient-first hook (2.22% ER) means you MUST lead with the mechanism. Never open with "pea protein" or "protein coffee."

---

### Cinnamon
**Don't say:** "Cinnamon for flavor and metabolism"
**Do say:** "Cinnamon is in here for one reason: blood sugar stability. Specifically, to hold the floor between 9am and noon. Without it, the matcha + coffee combination peaks and drops. With it, the curve stays flat."
**Best platform:** Instagram (P2 science), Shopify blog
**Angle that works:** The "not for flavor" framing surprises people. It signals that every ingredient is intentional — which builds formula credibility for the whole stack.

---

### Ginkgo Biloba ⭐ (proven performer)
**Don't say:** "Ginkgo for focus and memory"
**Do say:** "The tree that survived the Hiroshima blast. 270 million years old. One of the most studied plants in cognitive science. 120mg of standardized extract in every scoop."
**Best platform:** Instagram Reel (P2 science), X
**Angle that works:** Historical survival claim → curiosity → what it does → the dose. This hook hit 10.53% ER — the best in the Sales Motor period. The pattern: surprising claim about the ingredient's existence, not its effect.
**Important:** After the hook, explain the mechanism. "Supports blood circulation to the brain. The quality of your focus is partly a blood flow problem."

---

### Ginger
**Don't say:** "Ginger for digestion"
**Do say:** "Ginger is in here because some people can't do coffee on an empty stomach. It reduces the gut friction so the formula works at 5am without food."
**Best platform:** Instagram (P2 detail), FAQ content
**Angle that works:** Problem-solution (gut sensitivity with morning coffee), not the thermogenic angle. The thermogenic claim is harder to feel and less distinctive.

---

### L-Carnitine
**Don't say:** "L-Carnitine for fat burn"
**Do say:** "Your body makes L-carnitine. Your brain uses it to convert fat into fuel. Most coffees skip it. We didn't."
**Best platform:** X, Instagram Carousel
**Angle that works:** The "your body already does this" frame makes it feel natural, not supplemental. Positions carnitine as a support ingredient, not a fat-loss pill.

## Cross-Ingredient Narratives (most powerful for long-form)

### The Double Crash Explainer
"Most people think the 10am crash is caffeine wearing off. It's not — caffeine's half-life is 5 hours, it's still in your system. What crashes is blood sugar. When caffeine triggers cortisol, blood glucose temporarily spikes. 2–3 hours later, it drops. That's the crash.

Power Coffee addresses this in two ways:
- **Pea protein** (5g) slows the glucose drop
- **Cinnamon** (500mg) improves insulin sensitivity, keeping the floor higher

The energy doesn't just peak later. The floor holds."

Best for: email, Shopify blog, LinkedIn

---

### The Stack vs. Single Scoop Angle
"Other people's morning ritual: coffee + L-theanine pills + protein shake + fish oil + ginkgo capsule + a vitamin.

Power Coffee: one scoop. All of it in there. $1.19 a morning."

Best for: Instagram Reel hook, X post

We tell you:

Nothing hidden. Nothing at a label claim."

Best for: Instagram Carousel, website, investor deck

## What NOT to Do (confirmed failures)

| Mistake | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Lead with ingredient name | "THERE'S PROTEIN IN YOUR COFFEE" = 2.22% ER | Lead with the mechanism or the pain |
| "Boost", "supercharge", "maximize" | Triggers supplement skepticism | Use "holds", "stable", "smooth" |
| "Sustained energy" | Brand language, not buyer language | Use "level energy" or "no drop" |
| "Clinically proven" | FTC violation risk | Use "research suggests" / "associated with" |
| "Did you know…" opening | Worst carousel opener in SM period (1.30% ER) | Use declarative statements |
| Faith-forward on cold audience | Works for retention, wrong for cold acquisition | Reserve for warm/community content |

## Buyer Language to Use (from PULSE data, May 2026)

Instead of... → Use...
- "sustained energy" → "level energy" / "smooth energy"
- "afternoon crash" → "by 2pm I crash" (specific time is more powerful)
- "cognitive clarity" → "focus without feeling scattered"
- "mental performance" → "when simple things feel harder than they should"
- "optimize" → "fits into my routine"

**The #1 hook word from buyers: "smooth."**
Buyers use it for taste AND energy experience. Whenever possible, use "smooth" over any other descriptor.
