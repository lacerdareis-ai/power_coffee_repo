# Welcome Video Script — New Clients
**Format:** Leo to camera | **Duration:** ~2:20 | **Tone:** Warm, direct, personal

---

## Setup Notes

**Location:** Home kitchen — lived-in, real. Same setting as the brand's morning content.
**Wardrobe:** Clean but casual. Not a pitch outfit.
**Props:** One pack of Power Coffee on the counter. One mug. A scoop. Almond milk carton visible.
**Time of day:** Morning light. If possible, film early.
**Delivery:** Calm and unhurried. Leo is not selling. He is welcoming someone into something he already believes in.

---

## Script

---

### OPENING — Welcome (0–15s)

"If you're watching this, you just ordered your first pack of Power Coffee.

I wanted to be the one to walk you through it — personally.

I'm Leo. I built this formula. And I want to take two minutes to tell you why it exists and exactly how to use it."

---

### THE STORY — How It Was Created (15–60s)

"A few years ago I was trying back training triathlon, after move to us.

My mornings were dialed — 4:45am, training done before my kids woke up. But by 2pm I was gone. Three coffees deep, couldn't hold a thought. I was crashing at exactly the moment my family needed me to show up.

So I started asking: what is coffee actually missing?

It took me two years and a lot of failed batches to get this right. Seven ingredients — matcha for a longer energy arc, taurine to prevent overstimulation, pea protein for blood sugar stability, ginkgo, cinnamon, ginger, and a clean base of instant coffee. No sugar. No fillers. Nothing you can't pronounce.

This isn't a supplement. It's an upgrade to the ritual you already have."

---

### HOW TO USE — Two Methods (60–110s)

"Now, there are two ways I love to use it — and both work great.

**The first:** with almond milk and honey.

One scoop in eight to ten ounces of warm almond milk — not boiling, just warm. Half a teaspoon of honey if you want a little sweetness. Stir well, give it about thirty seconds.

It tastes like a smooth, clean latte. That's it."

*[Leo demonstrates — scoops, stirs, holds up the mug. Relaxed.]*

"**The second way** — and this is actually how I use it most mornings — is to add one scoop directly into your regular brew. Your drip coffee, your espresso, your French press, whatever you already make. Just stir it in.

You keep your ritual exactly the same. You just make it work a lot harder.

Either way — one scoop is the serving. You don't need more."

---

### WHAT TO EXPECT — The Day 3 Moment (110–130s)

"One thing I want to set your expectations on: Day 1 is not always dramatic. And that's okay.

The people who get the most out of Power Coffee give it three days.

On Day 3 is usually when it clicks — the energy holds longer, the afternoon holds, and you realize you didn't reach for a second cup.

That's the signal. That's when you know it's working."

---

### CLOSE — Personal Sign-Off (130–140s)

"I still drink this every single morning — over 550 mornings in. Same formula. Same result.

If you have any questions, reply to your order confirmation email. It comes directly to me.

Welcome to the ritual. I hope it does for you what it does for me."

*[Leo looks into the lens. Small nod. Holds the mug.]*
