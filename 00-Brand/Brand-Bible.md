# Power Coffee — Brand Bible

## Brand Identity

**Tagline:** The first win of your day.

**Positioning:** Clean, functional energy that replaces your morning coffee ritual with something that actually works — sustained focus, no crash, no jitters.

**Voice:** Calm confidence. Not hype. Not bro-science. Think: the friend who's already winning at 6am and tells you exactly what he drinks.

**Tone keywords:** Direct, warm, science-backed, ritual-focused, aspirational but grounded.

**What we are NOT:** We are not a supplement brand. We are not a gym brand. We are a daily ritual brand that happens to have functional ingredients.

## Product Specs

| Field | Detail |

### Core Ingredients
- Instant coffee (base)
- Matcha (sustained energy + L-theanine calm)
- Taurine (focus + hydration)
- Pea protein (satiety + muscle support)
- Cinnamon (blood sugar regulation + flavor)
- Ginger (anti-inflammatory + digestion)
- Ginkgo biloba (under consideration — cognitive support)

### Key Claims (compliant language)
- "Designed for sustained energy without the crash"
- "Functional ingredients chosen for focus and clarity"
- "Clean formula — no artificial sweeteners, no fillers"
- "The daily ritual that powers your morning"

### What NOT to claim
- No medical claims (does not treat/cure/prevent)
- No "boosts testosterone" or hormonal claims
- No "clinically proven" unless we have specific studies
- No comparison to medications or prescriptions

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary: The Ambitious Professional
- Age 28-45, male/female
- Health-conscious but time-poor
- Already drinks coffee daily (2-3 cups)
- Interested in optimization, productivity, biohacking
- Income $60K-$120K
- Shops on Amazon and DTC brands
- Active on Instagram, listens to podcasts

### Secondary: The Fitness-Minded Parent
- Age 30-45, male/female
- Trains 3-4x/week
- Wants clean energy without pre-workout harshness
- Values family health, reads labels
- Willing to pay premium for quality ingredients

### Pain Points We Solve
1. "I crash after my second coffee by 2pm"
2. "Pre-workout is too much, regular coffee isn't enough"
3. "I want something functional but it has to taste good"
4. "I don't have time for a complicated morning supplement stack"
