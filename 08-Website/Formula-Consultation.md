## Research-backed dose recommendations for the remaining 3g

### Instant Coffee — 1,500mg (1.5g)
**Caffeine yield:** ~40–55mg depending on grade of soluble coffee used.
**Why this dose:** Combined with matcha caffeine (~12–15mg from 600mg matcha), total caffeine per serving is ~55–70mg — a mild, functional dose designed to pair with a regular coffee (where you add the scoop on top). If used standalone, it's still sufficient to anchor the formula without overstimulating.
**Research baseline:** Cognitive performance benefits begin at 40mg caffeine; diminishing returns and jitter risk climb above 200mg.

### Matcha Green Tea — 600mg
**Caffeine yield:** ~12–15mg (matcha is ~2–3% caffeine by weight).
**L-Theanine from matcha:** ~9–12mg naturally occurring.
**Why this dose:** In a constrained blend, 500–750mg delivers meaningful natural L-theanine and slow-release caffeine alongside the additional supplemental L-theanine. Going higher (1g+) starts displacing other ingredients without proportionate benefit in a stack.

### L-Theanine (supplemental, added separately) — 200mg
**Why this dose:** Research from Haskell et al. (2008) shows the L-theanine + caffeine combination significantly improves attention-switching accuracy and alertness vs. caffeine alone. The effect holds at 100–200mg L-theanine with 50–100mg caffeine. Adding 200mg supplemental on top of what matcha contributes naturally (~10mg) gives reliable, consistent delivery regardless of matcha batch variability.
**Real total L-theanine per serving:** ~210mg (200mg supplemental + ~10mg from matcha).

### Ginkgo Biloba (standardized extract) — 120mg
**Standard:** EGb 761 extract, standardized to 24% flavone glycosides, 6% terpene lactones.
**Why this dose:** 120mg is the most replicated clinical dose in cognitive function trials. Studies showing improvements in memory, processing speed, and cerebral blood flow consistently used 120–240mg. At 120mg in a multi-ingredient stack, you're at minimum effective dose.

### Cinnamon (Ceylon preferred) — 500mg
**Why this dose:** The blood sugar mechanism you're using in content ("keeps the floor from dropping at 10am") is well-supported at 500mg–2g per day. In a blend context, 500mg is the minimum meaningful dose for the insulin sensitivity pathway. Use Ceylon (true cinnamon) over Cassia — same mechanism, lower coumarin risk at daily use.

### Ginger Root Extract — 250mg
**Why this dose:** Thermogenic and anti-inflammatory effects documented at 250mg–1g. In a functional coffee blend where ginger plays a supporting thermogenic role (not the primary ingredient), 250mg hits the mechanism without dominating flavor. Standardized to 5% gingerols if extracting for potency.

## Formula verdict

**The stack is well-designed.** The pea protein + cinnamon combination for blood sugar management alongside taurine for focus modulation is genuinely differentiated — most competitors don't do this. The weak point is caffeine dose: at 55–70mg total, standalone use is mild. This is actually a feature (no jitter risk, no anxiety) but could confuse buyers who expect a strong caffeine hit. The "add to existing coffee" positioning solves it cleanly.

**One recommended upgrade:** If any reformulation happens, consider raising L-Theanine to 250mg and dropping Ginger to 150mg. The L-theanine effect compounds on the brand's core promise (no crash, no jitter) more directly than ginger's thermogenic role.
