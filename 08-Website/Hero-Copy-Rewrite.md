## Notes on CTA
- Pick ONE price framing and stick with it across the page
- "$1.19 a morning" is more compelling than "$17.90 for 7 days" — it makes the decision feel small
- Drop emoji (👉) from CTAs — undermines the brand's calm, confident voice
- "BOOST MY DAY" — too hype. Drop it.
