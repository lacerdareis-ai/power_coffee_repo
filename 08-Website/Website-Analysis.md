# Power Coffee vs Saltivate — Website Improvement Analysis
_Generated 2026-06-16_

## The 5 critical gaps

### 1. Ingredient transparency — the biggest miss
Saltivate shows `800mg Sodium | pulls water into your bloodstream and helps you hold onto it`.
Power Coffee says "research-backed, recommended dosages" but never shows a single number.
You claim "real doses" in every content post — the website should prove it.
**Fix:** Ingredient transparency section with each ingredient, its dose, and its mechanism.

### 2. "A.I. science formula" is a trust killer
This is the single worst line on the site. It sounds like a GPT-3 chatbot generated the formula.
Saltivate's credibility: third-party testing, HSA/FSA eligible, specific dosages per ingredient.
Power Coffee's credibility: "A.I. science formula" — which signals the opposite of what you intend.
**Fix:** Kill this phrase. Replace with "11 real ingredients, real doses. Nothing at a label claim."

### 3. The $17.90 vs $30 angle isn't on the homepage
This is your biggest differentiator and your best content angle. It's not on the site visually.
Saltivate leads with their sharpest differentiator in the hero ("High Sodium, Zero Sugar").
**Fix:** Price comparison section showing $17.90 vs $28-$42 with the "why" explained.

### 4. Three competing CTAs, three different framings
"Try it for 7 days — $17.90" vs "BOOST MY DAY for $1.19/morning" vs "Start My 15-Day Supply"
These describe three different things. A confused buyer doesn't convert.
**Fix:** One primary CTA. One framing. Commit to either $17.90 or $1.19/morning, not both.

### 5. Hero headline underperforms
Current: "Eliminate Fatigue. Activate Clean, All-Day Energy – The Power Coffee"
This sounds like every supplement brand. "Eliminate fatigue" is generic.
**Fix:** Lead with the contrarian claim that matches your content strategy (see below).
