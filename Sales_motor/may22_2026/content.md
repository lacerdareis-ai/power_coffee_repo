### Body
## The First Batch Model

When The Power Coffee launched, the founding batch was priced lower than standard — intentionally. The logic was simple: asking someone to pay full price for a new formula, from a brand they've never heard of, with no reviews and no track record, is a different ask than selling something with a proven history.

First batch pricing was for the people willing to take that bet early. This Sunday, that window closes.

But before it does, it's worth explaining what the formula actually is — because the price changing does not mean the formula changes.

## What 11 Ingredients Actually Means

Most functional products are built around one or two hero ingredients and padded out with trace amounts of supporting compounds. They list 11 things on the label; 9 of them are below any dosage shown in research to produce an effect.

The Power Coffee was built differently. Every ingredient is at a dose with a documented mechanism.

**Caffeine at ~175mg** is the primary driver — sourced from both coffee and matcha, which gives a different absorption profile than coffee alone. The matcha fraction has a slower onset and a longer tail, extending the energy window naturally.

**L-theanine at 200mg** is the compound that most noticeably changes the caffeine experience. Multiple trials show the combination reduces anxiety and improves sustained attention compared to caffeine alone. It doesn't reduce the stimulation — it changes the shape of it.

**Taurine at 2 grams** is the dose used in serious neurological research, not the 200mg trace amount used in energy drinks for marketing purposes. Taurine modulates calcium signaling in neurons and interacts with GABA receptors, stabilizing the neural electrical environment while caffeine is raising overall excitation.

**Ginkgo biloba** supports cerebrovascular circulation — blood flow to the brain. The result is a more sustained and evenly distributed cognitive effect throughout a long work session, rather than a front-loaded peak that drops off.

**Pea protein at 5g** is the addition that surprises people most. Caffeine asks the body to perform. Without any macronutrient substrate, you're stimulating a system with nothing to run on. Protein removes one of the physical mechanisms behind the crash.

## What First Batch Customers Have Said

The most consistent feedback we've received is about the absence of something: the crash that doesn't come. People describe one cup being enough — not because the energy is weaker, but because it holds. They stop needing a second stimulation cycle to compensate for the first one burning out.

That experience is what the formula was designed to produce. It is not an accident or an edge case. It is the predictable output of a complete system.
