## SHOPIFY BLOG
### Title
Why Your Morning Coffee Stops Working After 90 Minutes (And What to Do About It)

### Body
## The Crash Is Not Your Fault

Most people have made peace with the 10am wall. You drink your coffee, you feel sharp for an hour or so, and then the fog returns — sometimes heavier than before. You reach for a second cup. It helps a little. By 3pm, you're running on caffeine debt and habit.

The standard explanation is that you need more sleep, more water, or a better coffee. But if you've optimized all of those and the wall is still there, the problem isn't the input — it's the design of the tool.

## What Standard Coffee Actually Does

Caffeine's primary mechanism is adenosine receptor blockade. Adenosine is the compound that builds up in your brain throughout the day, signaling tiredness. Caffeine temporarily blocks that signal — which is genuinely useful.

But that's all it does.

Standard coffee doesn't replenish the neurotransmitter systems (dopamine, acetylcholine, norepinephrine) that caffeine burns through to produce alertness. It doesn't stabilize blood sugar, so the spike in glucose caused by caffeine on an empty stomach is followed by a corresponding drop. And without L-theanine — the compound found naturally in green tea — there's nothing to modulate the sharp, anxious quality of the caffeine signal.

The result: a spike, a jitter, and a predictable crash within 60–90 minutes.

## Why You Adapted Instead of Questioning It

Energy drinks and the third wave coffee industry both optimized for the peak, not the duration. Higher caffeine doses, better extraction, premium origins. All of that is interesting, but none of it solves the crash — because the crash is a formulation gap, not a caffeine quality issue.

Most people adapted their routines around it. Second coffee by 9:30. Third by noon. The industry sold more product. Nobody named the actual problem.

## What a Better-Designed Morning Looks Like

Fixing the crash means addressing multiple failure modes simultaneously:

**L-theanine** smooths caffeine's sharp edge by increasing GABA activity and reducing caffeine-induced cortisol spikes. The result is alertness without anxiety.

**Pea protein** slows gastric emptying, which blunts the blood sugar swing that underlies the second layer of the crash — the one that hits even when the adenosine rebound hasn't arrived yet.

**Cinnamon** improves insulin sensitivity and helps maintain glucose stability for longer after consumption.

**Taurine** at 2g works as a counter-regulator to caffeine's cardiovascular load, reducing strain while preserving the alertness effect.

**Matcha** contributes a secondary, slower caffeine curve and additional L-theanine.

Each of these isn't an added bonus. Each one closes a specific gap that standard coffee leaves open.

## The Point

You don't need to drink less coffee. You need a formula that was designed to last.

The Power Coffee was built around this exact problem — 11 ingredients, each with a functional role, formulated so that the energy you feel at 7:30am is still working at 10:30am.

If you're still rebuilding your morning around a crash, the crash is the thing worth fixing.
