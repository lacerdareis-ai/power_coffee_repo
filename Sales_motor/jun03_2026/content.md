## SHOPIFY BLOG
### Title
Why I Built The Power Coffee — The Founder's Honest Account

### Body
## The Problem I Stopped Ignoring

For the better part of a year, I was productive on paper.

Meetings attended, decisions made, output delivered. From the outside, it looked like a working routine. From the inside, it was a different story: three coffees before noon, a reliable crash somewhere between 1:30 and 2pm, and then a long grind through the back half of the day on stubbornness alone.

I told myself this was the cost of building something. That anyone running a company operates this way. That the afternoon drain was just what high-output work felt like.

Then I stopped believing that — and started going through the research.

## What Six Months of Research Actually Looks Like

I am not a formulator. I have no background in nutrition science or supplement chemistry. What I had was a specific problem, access to the research literature, and a very low tolerance for ingredients that exist to look good on a label rather than do something inside the body.

Every ingredient I considered had to meet three criteria: a plausible mechanism, a clinically studied dose, and a reason to be there that was not "it's trending." Eleven ingredients made it through that filter.

Taurine at 2 grams — the dose studied for mitochondrial support, not the cosmetic 200mg used by most products. L-theanine at 100mg to smooth the caffeine curve and reduce the jitteriness that comes from stimulant use alone. Ginkgo biloba for cerebrovascular circulation, which affects how well your brain actually uses the oxygen and nutrients it receives. Pea protein at 5 grams to prevent the blood sugar spike-and-crash that follows a caffeine-only morning. Matcha alongside the coffee for a slower, layered caffeine release rather than a single sharp peak.

The other ingredients fill in the remaining gaps. The formula is not complicated. It is just complete.

## The Moment the Formula Proved Itself

I was not looking for a dramatic result. I was looking for the absence of a problem.

The moment I knew the formula was right: 3pm on a Tuesday. I was on a call — the kind of late-afternoon call I would typically have been half-present for, managing fatigue in the background while trying to engage in the foreground. On this particular Tuesday, I was just there. Fully present. No crash running underneath the conversation. No willpower tax being paid in real time.

That was it. Not a spike, not a rush, not a performance enhancement in the gym-supplement sense. Just a Tuesday afternoon that felt like a Tuesday afternoon should.

## What This Was Always About

The Power Coffee exists because I built it to solve my own problem — and it did.

Every morning I mix a scoop, I am not promoting a product. I am using the thing I spent six months building for myself. That is the only honest baseline I can offer: this is the formula I made because I needed it, and I still need it, and it still works.

If your current energy setup is getting you to 10am but not to 3pm — the formula, the doses, and what is actually happening inside the ingredients you are consuming are worth a serious look.
