## Ad Copy Patterns That Work

| Pattern | Example | Why It Works | Date Found |
|---|---|---|---|
| Present-tense problem scene | "Two cups in. Still foggy." | Puts viewer inside their own pain in 5 words. No setup needed. #4 all-time on IG (11 ix, 1.17% ER, 3.7/day). | May 2026 |
| Price math comparison | "$0.73 a morning. Real focus. No crash." | Reframes cost against daily latte spend. Disarms price objection before it forms. | May 2026 |
| Pain-first ingredient reveal | "YOUR 11AM CRASH ISN'T CAFFEINE. IT'S BLOOD SUGAR." → then reveal ingredient | Lead with the problem the viewer already feels, use the ingredient as the solution reveal in slide 2. Outperforms leading with the ingredient name directly. | May 2026 |
| Nuclear/survival hook for ingredients | "The tree that survived a nuclear blast is in your coffee." | Surprising historical claim drives curiosity. Ginkgo carousel hit 4–6 likes vs protein carousel ("THERE'S PROTEIN IN YOUR COFFEE") at 1 like same age. | May 2026 |
| Surprising claim hook > direct ingredient name | Ginkgo "nuclear blast" carousel: 10.53% ER. Pea protein "THERE'S PROTEIN IN YOUR COFFEE": 2.22% ER. Same format, same pillar, 4.7x difference. | Never lead with the ingredient name. Lead with the claim, the history, or the mechanism. The ingredient is the payoff, not the hook. | May 4–22, 2026 |
| Founder daily-use proof | "430 mornings of drinking my own formula. Not for content." | Specificity (430 days) + anti-marketing framing ("not for content") drives credibility. Outperforms generic founder claims. | May 13, 2026 |
| P3 Reel — Ironman/family angle | "Ironman prep means two hours before the day starts. Then the workday. Then Gabriel and Helena need a dad who's present." | Real context + real stakes. May 20 Reel: 438 views, 279 reach, 3.94% ER — highest reach post in Sales Motor period. | May 20, 2026 |

### Template entries:
- **Price anchor hook:** "$0.73 vs $5.75" — comparison to daily coffee shop spend
- **Problem-first hook:** "Your coffee is failing you by 2pm" — names the pain immediately
- **Ingredient spotlight:** "What matcha does that caffeine can't" — education-based
- **Founder story:** "I built this because..." — authenticity and trust
- **Math/logic:** "Let's do the math" — appeals to analytical buyers
