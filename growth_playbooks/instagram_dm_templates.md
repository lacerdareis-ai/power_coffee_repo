## Key Phrases That Work

**"We're a local brand"** = Trust signal (not a faceless corporation)
**"Really want to"** = Shows genuine interest, not transactional
**"Think your customers would vibe/dig it"** = Shows you understand their audience
**"No strings attached / No pitch"** = Lowers their guard, builds trust

---

## Dos & Don'ts

✅ **DO:**
- Lead with "local Boston brand" (builds connection)
- Keep it 2-3 sentences max
- End with a question (engagement)
- Use their specific vibe/content as proof you know them
- Show confidence in the product

❌ **DON'T:**
- Make it about you ("we're trying to grow")
- Use generic "check us out"
- Ask for sale immediately
- Use too many emojis
- Make promises ("will sell great")
