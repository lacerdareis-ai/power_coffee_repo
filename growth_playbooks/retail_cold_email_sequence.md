# Retail Cold-Email Sequence — Power Coffee

**Target:** Boutique gyms, wellness shops, specialty food retailers in New England (Boston area first).
**Goal:** Wholesale/consignment stocking or sampling placement.
**Status:** 1 email sent (Stand Farm Shop, Jun 30). Sequence not yet systematized.
**Skill:** Use `/cold-email` when adapting for new targets.

---

## Research checklist before sending to any store

- Find the owner's name (not just "store@")
- Know their Instagram account and what they currently carry
- Note whether they sell functional foods / supplements already
- Check if they have a coffee station, fitness crowd, or wellness-forward customer base
- Mention one specific thing from their store/IG in Email 1

---

## Email 1 — The Opener (Day 1)

**Subject:** Quick question about your morning customers

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Store Name] on Instagram — the [specific thing: product launch / event photo / store aesthetic] caught my attention.

I'm Leo, founder of The Power Coffee. We make a functional coffee for people who take their mornings seriously: 11 ingredients, 175mg caffeine, 5g protein, no crash. $18.90 for 15 servings. Made in USA.

We've built a real following in Boston and our customer base overlaps directly with yours: performance-focused adults who read labels and want something that works.

I'm not pitching a distributor deal. I'm asking whether you'd be open to a quick call or a sample box so you can try it yourself first.

Would a 5-minute call this week work, or should I just send samples?

Leo Lacerda, Founder
The Power Coffee
thepowercoffee.com

## Wholesale terms to have ready

- **Consignment option:** Supply on consignment for first 30 days, no upfront cost to retailer
- **Wholesale price:** ~$11-12/unit (retailer marks up to $18.90-$22.00)
- **Minimum first order:** 12 units
- **Shelf life:** 12 months from production
- **Materials provided:** POS card, product card with QR to website, sample sachets for staff
- **Reorder terms:** Net-30 after first consignment period validates
