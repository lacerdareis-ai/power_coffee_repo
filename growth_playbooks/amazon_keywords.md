## Amazon PPC Campaign Structure

### Campaign 1: Core Problem (High Intent)
- Keywords: Tier 1 + top Tier 2
- Daily budget: $300-400
- Bid strategy: Manual (control bids per tier)
- Target: 2.5-3.5% ACoS
- Scaling: Increase winner keywords by 20% weekly

### Campaign 2: Competitor Capture
- Keywords: Tier 2
- Daily budget: $150-200
- Bid strategy: Manual, aggressive on top 3 competitors
- Target: 3-4% ACoS
- Scaling: Pause underperformers (>5% ACoS) after 100 clicks

### Campaign 3: Awareness/Discovery
- Keywords: Tier 3 + Tier 4 long-tail
- Daily budget: $150-200
- Bid strategy: Automatic (Amazon sets bids)
- Target: 2-3% ACoS (high volume = lower conversion, but scale)
- Scaling: Identify top 5 keywords, move to Campaign 1

### Campaign 4: Broad Audience (Test)
- Keywords: Broad category terms ("functional coffee", "coffee supplements")
- Daily budget: $100-150
- Bid strategy: Automatic
- Target: 2-3% ACoS
- Scaling: Feed data back to Campaign 3

**Total Budget:** $700-950/day (~$21k-28.5k/month)

## Amazon Copy Optimization (Use in Listings)

### Title Formula
Include: [Main Benefit] + [Format] + [Brand]
Example: "Power Coffee - Functional Coffee Powder - No Crash Energy - 15 Servings"

### Bullet Points
1. **Lead with the problem:** "Solves the afternoon energy crash with clean, sustained focus"
2. **Ingredient credibility:** "Matcha + L-theanine + taurine + pea protein - 60+ ingredients"
3. **Format advantage:** "Instant - no mixing, no cleanup, grab and go"
4. **Social proof:** "300 people tested - 70% confirmed energy and focus increase"
5. **Usage:** "One scoop in your morning coffee for 4-6 hours of clean energy"
6. **Price value:** "$1.19 per serving - premium functional nutrition"

### Search Backend Keywords (Hidden)
- functional coffee
- energy coffee
- no crash coffee
- clean energy drink
- coffee supplement
- matcha coffee
- focus coffee
- sustainable coffee

## Amazon Review Strategy

**Incentive email (send within 48 hours of delivery):**

Subject: "Did Power Coffee boost your days?"

Body:
"Thanks for your order of Power Coffee! We'd love to know: did your energy or focus improve? Leave a review and tell us your experience. [Button: Write Review]

Bonus: Tag #BoostedDays in your review and we'll send you a free sample pack!"

**Review response templates:**

*5-star:*
"Thank you! This is exactly what we're building for — sustainable energy without the crash. Share your experience on Instagram @powercoffee.ofc and tag #BoostedDays for a chance to be featured!"

*3-4 star:*
"We're glad Power Coffee helped! Every review helps us improve. Quick question: what would make it a 5? Reply to this message and let's make it right."

*1-2 star:*
"We're sorry this didn't work for you. We stand behind our product. Please message us directly — we want to make this right. [Contact link]"

**Target:** 50+ reviews per month → 1,000 in 20 months at average 4.7+ stars

**Monday:** Review weekend data, pause underperformers (>5% ACoS for 50+ clicks)
