### Title
Why the Founder of The Power Coffee Drinks His Own Formula Every Single Morning

### Body

## Built for One Person First

Most supplement brands are built by marketers for a market segment.

The Power Coffee was built by someone with a problem — and the formula reflects that.

Leo Lacerda, founder of The Power Coffee, started building the formula in Fall 2024 after hitting a wall with his own energy routine. Three cups of coffee. A protein shake. A handful of nootropic capsules. Still crashing by 2pm.

## 18 Months of Self-Testing

Leo didn't outsource the proof-of-concept. He ran it on himself.

Five formula versions. Three taste panels. Hundreds of mornings adjusting ratios, tracking focus, measuring how long the energy lasted, and watching for the crash that never came with the final formula.

The process used AI tools to analyze research on ingredients like taurine, matcha, L-theanine, pea protein, and ginkgo biloba — combinations that had evidence behind them but had never been brought together in a single-scoop coffee booster.

## What 18 Months Confirms

After a year and a half of daily use, here's what holds up:

- **Sustained energy** — ~175mg of caffeine from clean sources, extended by matcha's L-theanine
- **No crash** — protein and adaptogens buffer the cortisol spike that typical coffee creates
- **Real focus** — ginkgo and taurine support cognitive performance without overstimulation
- **One step** — one scoop added to your existing coffee. No stack required.

## The Simplest Endorsement

Leo still drinks Power Coffee every morning. Not for the brand. Because stopping would mean going back to the problem it solved.

That's the most honest thing a founder can say about their product.
