## LINKEDIN
### Post

I spent two years asking one question: why does my energy always fall apart at 2pm?

The answer wasn't caffeine. I was already drinking three cups. More caffeine made the crash worse and the anxiety sharper.

The real problem was what regular coffee was missing.

When caffeine clears — roughly five hours after your first cup — the adenosine it was blocking floods back in at once. That's not tiredness. That's a mechanism. And you can design around it.

I started formulating around that gap. L-theanine from matcha to smooth the caffeine arc. Taurine as a neuromodulator, not a stimulant. Pea protein to stabilize blood sugar. Cinnamon for metabolic stability.

Seven ingredients. One scoop. Two years of batch testing.

I still drink it every morning before my family wakes — same formula, same result. Not because I have to. Because it works.

One way to know if it will work for you: add one scoop to your coffee tomorrow morning. See if you reach for a second cup by 10am.

Leo, Founder — The Power Coffee
