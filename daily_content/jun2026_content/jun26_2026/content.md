### Body

## The Morning Stack Problem

If you're serious about performance, you've probably tried to build a morning routine around energy and focus. Protein shake at 6am. Pre-workout or energy drink by 7. A nootropic capsule somewhere in there. Maybe a second coffee at 11.

It works — sort of. But it's expensive, time-consuming, and easy to skip when your morning gets compressed.

There's a simpler approach.

## What Functional Coffee Actually Does

Functional coffee isn't a replacement for your regular brew. It's a booster — a scoop of targeted ingredients you add to the coffee you're already making.

The concept works because coffee is already part of your routine. The friction of starting something new is nearly zero.

The difference is what's in the scoop.

## The 11-Ingredient Formula

Power Coffee combines 175mg of caffeine with matcha for a smoother, extended energy curve. L-theanine (present in matcha) pairs with caffeine to reduce jitteriness without dulling focus.

Taurine — 2g per serving — acts as a neuromodulator, helping regulate neural activity so you stay sharp without overstimulation.

Pea protein (5g) stabilizes blood sugar, which is what most regular coffees fail to address. Without protein, caffeine alone causes blood sugar swings that accelerate a crash.

Ginkgo biloba supports cerebral blood flow. Cinnamon helps manage post-meal glucose response.

Together, these ingredients don't fight each other. They work in sequence.

## The Practical Result

Most customers report no meaningful energy dip between their first cup at 6–7am and early afternoon. The second cup habit fades. The midday energy drink stops being necessary.

That's not a claim — that's what happens when blood sugar, caffeine metabolism, and neural regulation are all supported at once.

## One Scoop Is the Whole Stack

If you're spending $80–120/month on a combination of protein, nootropics, and energy products, Power Coffee consolidates that into one bag that lasts 30 servings.

Available now at thepowercoffee.com and on Amazon. If your mornings feel like a grind, this is the simplest upgrade you haven't tried yet.
