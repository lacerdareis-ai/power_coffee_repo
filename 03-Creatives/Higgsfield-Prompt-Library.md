## Product Photography Prompts

### PROD-01: Hero Shot (Clean Background)
**Model:** FLUX.2 Pro (image)
**Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (IG feed)
```
A premium coffee product pack with warm gold and dark brown branding, placed on a clean white marble surface. Soft morning sunlight from the left creates gentle shadows. The pack is slightly angled. A small pile of coffee powder spills artfully from the opening. Shallow depth of field. Minimalist, editorial product photography style. No text overlay.
```

### PROD-02: Ingredients Flat-Lay
**Model:** FLUX.2 Pro (image)
**Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (IG feed)
```
Overhead flat-lay photography of functional coffee ingredients arranged on a warm wooden cutting board. Small ceramic bowls containing: green matcha powder, ground cinnamon sticks, fresh ginger root, coffee beans, and a scoop of light beige pea protein powder. Natural morning light, soft shadows, warm color palette. Editorial food photography style. Premium and clean.
```

### PROD-03: Morning Mug Scene
**Model:** FLUX.2 Pro (image)
**Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (IG feed) and 9:16 (stories)
```
A steaming ceramic mug of coffee on a clean kitchen counter, morning golden hour light streaming through a window. A coffee product pack sits beside the mug. The scene feels warm, calm, and inviting. Shallow depth of field with bokeh background. Lifestyle product photography, premium DTC brand aesthetic.
```

## Lifestyle Photography Prompts

### LIFE-01: Morning Ritual — Kitchen
**Model:** FLUX.2 Pro (image)
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (stories/reels)
```
A person in their 30s standing in a modern kitchen at sunrise, pouring hot water into a mug. They are wearing a casual clean outfit. The scene is warm and calm — golden morning light fills the space. A coffee product pack is visible on the counter. The person looks focused and content. Natural, authentic lifestyle photography. Not overly staged.
```

### LIFE-02: Focused at Work — Desk Scene
**Model:** FLUX.2 Pro (image)

```
A person at a clean modern desk working on a laptop at 2pm (warm afternoon light). They look sharp, focused, and energized. A ceramic mug and a coffee product pack sit beside the laptop. Clean workspace, minimal decor. The feeling is "this person has sustained energy." Professional lifestyle photography.
```

A person in their 30s walking outdoors in morning light holding a to-go mug, looking energized and confident. Urban park or waterfront setting. Clean activewear but not gym clothes. The vibe is morning ritual meets clean energy. Natural, editorial lifestyle photography.

## Video Ad Prompts

### VID-01: Product Reveal (5-second loop)
**Model:** Kling 3.0 (video)
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (reels/stories)
**Duration:** 5 seconds
```
A coffee product pack slowly rotates on a clean marble surface as warm morning light sweeps across it. Steam rises gently from a mug in the background. Cinematic product reveal, slow motion, warm color grading. Premium DTC brand aesthetic.
```

### VID-02: Morning Ritual Sequence (10-second)
**Model:** Seedance 2.0 or Kling 3.0 (video)
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (reels)
**Duration:** 10 seconds
```
Quick-cut morning ritual sequence: hand reaches for a coffee product pack on a kitchen shelf, scoops powder into a mug, pours hot water, steam rises, person takes a sip and smiles with closed eyes. Warm morning light throughout. Natural, UGC-style filming aesthetic. Fast but smooth transitions.
```

### VID-03: Before/After Energy (15-second)
**Model:** Seedance 2.0 (video)
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (reels)
**Duration:** 15 seconds
```
Split narrative: First 7 seconds show a person sluggish at their desk in the afternoon, reaching for their third regular coffee. Cut to black. Then 8 seconds of the same person in the morning, energized and focused, with a branded coffee mug in hand. The contrast is clear — one coffee ritual works, the other doesn't. Warm color grading. Authentic, not overly produced.
```

### VID-04: Ingredient Callout (8-second)
**Model:** Kling 3.0 (video)
**Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (IG feed)
**Duration:** 8 seconds
```
Close-up sequence of individual ingredients being added to a mug in slow motion: a scoop of coffee powder, a dusting of green matcha, a pinch of cinnamon, all falling gracefully into the mug. Hot water pours in at the end and everything mixes. Macro food photography style. Beautiful, clean, satisfying to watch. Warm lighting.
```

## UGC-Style Prompts

### UGC-01: Testimonial Setup
**Model:** Soul 2 or Seedance 2.0 (video)
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (reels/stories)
**Duration:** 10 seconds
```
A real-looking person in their 30s holding a coffee product pack, talking directly to camera in a home kitchen. Morning light, casual outfit, authentic energy. They look like they're genuinely recommending something to a friend. Phone-filmed aesthetic with slight movement. Not polished — real and relatable.
```

### UGC-02: Unboxing
**Model:** Kling 3.0 (video)
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (reels)
**Duration:** 10 seconds
```
Hands opening a shipping package to reveal a coffee product pack inside. The hands pick it up, turn it around to show the label, then open the pack and smell the contents. Close-up, personal, phone-filmed style. Kitchen counter background. Authentic unboxing excitement without being over the top.
```
