### Customer Buying Process

**Awareness Stage:**

- *Activities:* Feels unfocused or fatigued by 2 PM. Googles "how to avoid energy crash" or "healthy coffee alternatives." Sees content on productivity, mental fatigue, burnout.
- *Influencers:* LinkedIn creators talking about burnout, flow, or focus. Biohackers and health podcasters (Andrew Huberman, Mel Robbins). Peers mentioning they use something to stay productive.

**Consideration Stage:**

- *Activities:* Watches videos comparing mushroom coffee, nootropics, and supplements. Looks for products that combine taste, energy, and clean ingredients. Scrolls Instagram for real routines and reviews.
- *Influencers:* Wellness creators showing morning rituals (YouTube Shorts, Reels). Supplement reviewers and fitness coaches. Friends or coworkers recommending "just try this in your coffee."

**Decision Stage:**

- *Activities:* Visits The Power Coffee site or receives a free sample. Compares ingredients, pricing, and benefits with others (like Everyday Dose). Looks for simplicity: "Can I just mix this and go?"
- *Influencers:* Product review videos or UGC (unboxing, routine setup). Email with compelling testimonials. Peer validation or comments on content.

**Purchase Stage:**

- *Activities:* Clicks through product page or QR code on sample kit. Buys intro pack or subscribes after day 3 of ritual. Shares feedback or posts story with their cup.
- *Influencers:* Strong email/DM follow-up. Reorder reminder with motivational tip. First-time buyer discount or personal note.

## 2. Marketing Insights

### Brochure Insights

**Changes to Solution Description:**

- Focus messaging on "boost your existing coffee" instead of replacing it
- Simplify language around benefits: Energy, Focus, Metabolism
- Position the product more as a morning productivity ritual than just a supplement
- Include a before/after comparison: life with and without Power Coffee

**Features to Add/Modify/Delete:**

- *Add:* A visual showing how to use it in 3 easy steps
- *Add:* 7-day starter ritual option on product page
- *Modify:* Use more user-friendly headlines and visual hierarchy
- *Delete:* Any reference to medical claims or overly technical ingredient explanations

**Product Positioning Changes:**

- From "functional coffee" → to "Smart Energy for Your Routine"
- Frame it as a performance ritual, not just an energy booster
- Highlight clean-label, lactose-free, and jitter-free benefits
- Add emotional triggers: "Say goodbye to burnout" or "Finally focus — all day."
