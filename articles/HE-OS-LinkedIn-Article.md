## I Built a Marketing Department of One. It Runs at 4am. I Call It HE-OS.

Nine months ago I was a solo founder drowning in my own marketing.

One person. No agency. No team. A functional coffee brand that needed to show up every day on Instagram, X, LinkedIn, email, and more — while I was also formulating product, talking to customers, and training for an Ironman before the sun came up.

Something had to give. Most founders in that spot hire an agency or burn out. I did neither.

I built a system. I call it **HE-OS — the High Energy Operational System** — and I built it alongside AI, not as a gimmick, but as the actual operator of the brand.

### What HE-OS actually is

Not "I use AI to write captions." An operating system that runs the marketing engine end to end:

- It **generates** the day's content across every platform — grounded in our real brand voice and the exact language our customers use.
- It **produces the visuals** — branded carousels and stories, on-brand every time.
- It **reviews its own work** against a quality bar before anything goes out.
- It **publishes** — Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Telegram, WhatsApp, the blog, email — automatically.
- Then every week it **reads the results and rewrites its own playbook**, so the next week is sharper than the last.

I don't write captions anymore. I don't schedule posts. The brand shows up every single morning at 4am — whether I'm asleep, traveling, or out on a ride.

### Does it work? The data — honestly.

I'll be straight, because being straight turns out to be our highest-performing move.

**Transparency beats polish.** The single best-performing thing we've published wasn't a product shot. It was me saying: *"I'm building this company with AI, and I'm not hiding it."* → **10.19% engagement rate — the most comments of any post in the period.** Same week, the polished carousel on the same topic did 1.3%. People reward the confession over the commercial.

**The system leans into what actually travels.** Our data showed short founder-POV video out-reaches static posts by roughly **8 to 1** — so HE-OS prioritizes it. One unscripted clip — *"I didn't even write a script for this one"* — hit **16.2% engagement** with the most comments we'd seen.

**It learns which ideas spread.** A counter-intuitive hook, *"Caffeine doesn't give you energy,"* opened at a **122% engagement rate on day one.** An ingredient framed as *"the tree that survived a nuclear blast"* hit **10.53%.** HE-OS caught those patterns and now reuses the *mechanism*, not the line.

These aren't million-view vanity numbers. We're an early brand growing on purpose. But every one of those signals was found, tested, and scaled by a system — not a team of ten.

### What it costs

Less than a part-time intern. A fraction of an agency retainer.

That's the part I want other founders to sit with: the old constraint — choose between speed and quality, or raise money just to afford a marketing team — is quietly dissolving.

### The real lesson

I didn't build HE-OS to remove myself from the brand. I built it to remove the *busywork*, so the only thing I spend my hours on is the human part — the story, the discipline, the life I'm actually living and documenting.

A solo founder can now operate like a funded team. Not in theory. Every morning at 4am.

If you're building something alone and treating *"I can't afford a team"* as your ceiling — I'd gently push back. The team might just be a system you haven't built yet.

What's the first thing you'd hand off to it?

**Leo Lacerda — Founder, The Power Coffee**
